# Founder Roles: One Question

This document outlines the key roles and responsibilities of the founders for the One Question project.

## Asym

**Role:** Founder & CEO

**Responsibilities:**

-   **Product Vision:** Defining the overall direction and long-term strategy of the One Question platform.
-   **Engineering:** Overseeing the technical development and implementation of the product.
-   **AI Systems:** Leading the design and development of AI-powered Q&A functionalities.
-   **Technical Architecture:** Establishing and maintaining the core technical structure and scalability of the platform.

## Paraja

**Role:** Founding Product Lead & Operations

**Responsibilities:**

-   **Creator Outreach:** Building relationships and engaging with content creators to onboard them onto the platform.
-   **Growth:** Developing and executing strategies for user acquisition and platform expansion.
-   **User Research:** Conducting research to understand user needs, behaviors, and pain points.
-   **Onboarding:** Designing and optimizing the user onboarding experience.
-   **Feedback Collection:** Establishing mechanisms for collecting, analyzing, and acting on user feedback.
-   **Launch Operations:** Managing the operational aspects of product launches and new feature rollouts.
